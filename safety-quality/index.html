01   Safety & quality

A formal system, tailored per contract.

Jeftoni has established a formal in-house Safety and Quality Management system which satisfies the broad cross section of Nigerian and other African States client requirements. This has earned the company various commendations.

03Certification

What Jeftoni does and does not claim.

  • Claimed

    Our Quality Management system is based upon ISO 9000 standards.

  • Claimed

    A quality plan tailored to the requirements of each specific contract.

  • Claimed

    A 189-record public register of executed work, with client, date and location for every job.

  • Not claimed

    No third-party ISO certification is asserted anywhere on this site, and no safety statistic is published, because neither has been independently stated.
