01 Position

Marine & Construction-Focused Engineering

JEFTONI Nig. Ltd., incorporated in June 1993, has built up her activities with highly qualified and experienced staff comprising indigenous Engineers and Technicians, equipped adequately with modern Geotechnical and Foundation Engineering experience and equipment to carry out works efficiently to satisfy clients. We undertake design and construction of Geotechnical and Foundation structures, supplying hidden strength to bridges, buildings, jetties, and other marine structures etc.
