01   Services

Capabilities

Conductor casing Piling, Bridge Design and Construction, Erecting Erosion Control Structures, Jetty Construction, shore line protection, Full range of Piling systems, Port and Harbour Construction and Maintenance, Pile Load testing, Bored piling, CFA piling, civil works, Facility Maintenance, Steel Fabrication, Flowline construction, Design and Fabrication of Drilling Augers, Drilling Buckets and kelly.

02Core

Six capabilities

Each one links to the projects in the register that evidence it — client, date and location included.

  • Conductor Piling Large-diameter drive pipe installed ahead of oil well drilling, supporting the upper sedimentary section of the well.
  • Full Range of Piling Systems Driven steel case, precast concrete, sheet, bored and CFA piling — 10″ to 30″ and 285 mm to 600 mm sections.
  • Jetty Construction & Berthing Facilities Jetties, quay walls and berthing facilities, with reclamation and soil improvement for yards and terminals.
  • Bridge Construction Foundations, substructures and superstructures for road and community bridges, plus facilities and civil works.
  • Soil & Geotechnical Investigation Soil investigation, laboratory testing, plate load testing and the design of geotechnical systems.
  • Steel Fabrication Bridge fabrication and assembly, pile fabrication, and in-house drilling augers, buckets and kelly bars.
